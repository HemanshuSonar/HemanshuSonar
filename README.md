## `~$ whoami`

I'm a Computer Engineering student at **A. P. Shah Institute of Technology**, with a Diploma in Computer Engineering from **Government Polytechnic, Thane** behind me. I like building full products end to end — frontend, backend, database, deployment — and I like it even more when something breaks in production and I have to go find out why.

Across diploma and engineering, I've built a fair spread of things — Android apps, marketplace platforms, an AI misinformation detector, and now an AI interview prep tool. **Elevo**, **SetuMitra** (a vendor-wholesaler marketplace), an artisan marketplace project, and **VerifAi** are the ones I'd point you to first.

Most days you'll find me somewhere between `build.log` and a Stack Overflow tab.
